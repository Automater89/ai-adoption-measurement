# Benchmark Reference

Target benchmarks used in this framework are grounded in real enterprise AI enablement program data, not industry survey averages.

---

## Benchmark Sources

| Benchmark | Source |
|---|---|
| Session completion rate 85%+ | Observed: 90%+ on high-volume tracks across 34-session program |
| Voluntary attendance 60%+ | Observed: 68%+ in mature program quarters |
| CSAT 2.8/3.0+ | Observed: 3.0/3.0 sustained across program |
| Skill conversion 70%+ | Observed: 72% average across cohorts |
| YoY voluntary learning hours +50% | Observed: +380% (exceptional; 50% is a realistic baseline target) |
| Feature adoption 65%+ | Industry reference: Microsoft 365 Copilot internal benchmarks |
| Repeat attendance 50%+ | Observed program data; programs below 40% show stall indicators |

---

## Interpreting Your Score

| Overall Score | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| 85-100 | Exceptional adoption. Program is self-sustaining. Focus on expansion and advanced use cases. |
| 70-84 | Strong adoption. One or two dimensions need attention. Investigate below-target metrics. |
| 55-69 | Moderate adoption. Engagement or effectiveness gaps present. Program redesign may be needed in weak areas. |
| 40-54 | Early-stage or stalling adoption. Likely a reach or motivation problem. Revisit program design and manager involvement. |
| Below 40 | Critical gaps. Consider pausing expansion until root causes are addressed. |

---

## Common Patterns

**High Reach, Low Effectiveness:** People are attending but not changing behavior. Likely cause: sessions are informational, not practice-based. Fix: add hands-on exercises and post-session accountability.

**High Effectiveness, Low Momentum:** Early adopters converted but growth has plateaued. Likely cause: program is not reaching new cohorts or creating peer advocates. Fix: peer champion program, manager-assigned nudges.

**Low Voluntary Attendance:** Most attendees are manager-mandated. Likely cause: program is not perceived as valuable. Fix: lead with pain points, not features. Show ROI in the first 10 minutes of every session.

**High CSAT, Low Feature Adoption:** People liked the training but are not using the tool. Likely cause: no workflow integration or permission barriers. Fix: identify the top 3 use cases for each role and build job aids.
